Know What You’re Booking With Santa Chimney Sweep

A dirty firebox, buildup inside the flue, and smoke entering the room can lead to different service requests. Give our team those details before booking so the appointment starts with the concern you actually have.

Confirm the Flue and Fireplace Scope

Tell us whether you have one fireplace or several, and ask which flues and cleaning areas the quote covers. Confirm whether firebox cleaning, inspection, or hardened-creosote removal is separate.

Report Smoke, Leaks, or Falling Pieces

If you see water staining, loose mortar, flue-tile fragments, or smoke coming into the room, mention it when you contact Santa Chimney Sweep. Removing soot will not repair damaged components.

Approve Additional Work Separately

Ask what was observed, whether it changes the cleaning scope, and what the proposed next step costs. Review any additional service before authorizing it.
