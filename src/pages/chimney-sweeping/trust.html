Know What You’re Booking With Santa Chimney Sweep

Santa Chimney Sweep helps homeowners understand their chimney's condition, the service being provided, and any additional concerns identified during the visit.

Clarify the Service Scope

Before work begins, homeowners can review which chimney areas and flues are covered by the requested service.

Chimney sweeping, fireplace cleaning, and formal inspections are different services, so the scope is clarified before proceeding.

Explain Additional Findings

If technicians discover damaged components, heavy creosote, or other problems, they explain the findings and why additional attention may be necessary.

Homeowners can understand the difference between routine cleaning and work that requires a separate inspection or repair.

Approve Additional Work Separately

If work beyond the original service is recommended, the team explains the proposed service and its scope before proceeding.

Homeowners have the opportunity to review the recommendation and approve additional work rather than having it automatically included in the original appointment.
