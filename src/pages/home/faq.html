How often should a chimney be inspected?

Inspection timing depends on the fireplace type, fuel, frequency of use, system condition, and recent service history. Arrange an inspection if you notice smoke, strong odors, moisture, falling debris, damaged masonry, or changes in how the fireplace vents. An inspection is also helpful when the chimney has not been checked recently or its service history is unknown.

What are the signs that a chimney may need repair?

Possible warning signs include cracked brick or mortar, water stains, damp odors, smoke entering the room, falling debris, damaged components near the roofline, or changes in fireplace performance. Because these conditions can have different causes, an inspection should come before the final chimney repair recommendation.
