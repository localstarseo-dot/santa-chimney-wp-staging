Chimney Construction FAQs

Questions Homeowners Ask About Chimney Construction

Clear answers about new chimney projects, masonry and brick construction, project costs, rebuilding, fireplaces, and service availability.

What is chimney construction?

Chimney construction means building a new chimney for a home, fireplace, or supported appliance.

The exact project depends on the property, chimney type, and what the chimney will serve.

When do I need a new chimney instead of chimney repair?

New construction is mainly for projects where a chimney does not already exist.

If you already have a damaged chimney, Chimney Repair , rebuilding, or restoration may make more sense.

Do you build masonry or brick chimneys?

Masonry and brick may be considered for some new chimney projects, depending on the home, project design, and intended use.

Tell us what you are planning so the available materials, construction type, and project fit can be confirmed before work moves forward.

Can you build a chimney for a new fireplace?

Possibly, depending on the fireplace, the home, and the venting requirements.

The fireplace and chimney should be reviewed as one project before the construction scope is confirmed. If the fireplace itself is the main project, see Fireplace Installation .

How much does chimney construction cost?

The cost can depend on chimney height, size, materials, roof conditions, access, the fireplace or appliance, flue or liner requirements, and the project’s overall complexity.

The property and proposed project need to be reviewed before an accurate estimate can be provided.

Is chimney construction the same as chimney rebuilding?

No. Chimney construction creates a new chimney where one does not currently exist. Chimney rebuilding reconstructs an existing damaged chimney.

See Chimney Rebuilding if an existing chimney needs major reconstruction.

Do you provide chimney construction outside Austin?

Santa Chimney Sweep serves Austin and approved nearby communities. Construction availability can depend on the property and project. Check our Service Areas page or call (512) 937-0590 to confirm availability for your location.
