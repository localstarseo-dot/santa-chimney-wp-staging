What Happens When You Plan a Fireplace Installation?

Santa Chimney Sweep reviews the proposed fireplace, existing setup, and installation requirements before defining the work. A replacement fireplace, new unit, and fireplace insert may each require a different approach.

Step 1

Review Your Project Goal

Our team begins by understanding what you want to install or change.

The review considers whether you are adding a new fireplace, replacing an existing unit, installing an insert, or upgrading the current system.

Your preferred appliance, existing setup, and available photos help establish the project's starting requirements.

01
Step 2

Check Fit and Compatibility

The selected fireplace must be compatible with the space and the venting arrangement it will use.

Important details include the fireplace opening, appliance dimensions, required clearances, chimney or vent condition, and fuel or electrical connections where applicable.

If the existing system is unsuitable or damaged, it may need additional work before installation can proceed.

02
Step 3

Define the Installation Scope

After reviewing the proposed system, the team identifies the installation work required for the project.

Depending on the approved scope, this may involve removing an existing unit, fitting a replacement appliance, installing an insert, or making changes to supported fireplace components.

Any required chimney modifications, gas connections, electrical work, framing, or finishing must be identified separately when they fall outside the approved installation scope.

03
Step 4

Review the Completed Setup and Handover

Once the approved installation work is finished, the completed setup is reviewed against the agreed scope and applicable appliance requirements.

Any required operating checks, manufacturer information, and instructions for the homeowner should be identified as part of the project handover.

If additional work or corrections are necessary, they should be explained before the project is considered complete.

04

What to Confirm Before Your Fireplace Installation

Review the selected appliance, compatibility, included installation work, and responsibilities before approving the project.

The proposed scope and responsibilities should be agreed before work begins.

Service Details to Confirm

  • Selected appliance and model
  • Opening dimensions and required clearances
  • Chimney or venting compatibility
  • Removal and installation scope
  • Gas, electrical, framing, and permit responsibilities
  • Operating checks and project handover

When Does Your Chimney Need More Than Sweeping?

Not every chimney problem can be solved by cleaning.

Heavy Creosote Buildup

Thick or hardened deposits may need specialized removal rather than routine sweeping.

Chimney Damage

Cracked liners, broken masonry, and other damaged components need evaluation and possible repair.

Explore Chimney Repair

Unknown Smoke Problems

If smoke enters your room, cleaning may help only when removable buildup is the cause.

Explore Chimney Inspections

Dirty Fireplace or Firebox

Fireplace cleaning focuses on ash and residue in the fireplace area. It is different from sweeping the chimney flue.

Understand Your Fireplace Installation Options

Santa Chimney Sweep helps homeowners understand which installation options fit their property, what the proposed work includes, and which requirements must be addressed before the project moves forward.

Review Compatibility Before Purchase

A fireplace must be selected based on more than its appearance or advertised features.

The existing opening, chimney, venting system, and manufacturer requirements help determine whether the proposed appliance is appropriate.

Reviewing compatibility before purchasing equipment can help avoid choosing a unit that will not fit the property.

Clarify Responsibilities

Some fireplace projects involve more than installing the appliance.

The proposed scope should identify who is responsible for the fireplace unit, removal of existing equipment, venting changes, gas or electrical connections, building modifications, and required approvals.

This helps homeowners understand which tasks are included and which need another qualified contractor.

Approve Changes Before Proceeding

If the installation requires work beyond the original plan, the team explains the additional requirements before proceeding.

Homeowners can review the proposed changes and approve the next steps based on the condition of the property and selected appliance.

Questions Homeowners Ask About Fireplace Installation

Can I install an insert in my existing fireplace?

Possibly. The existing fireplace opening, chimney condition, venting system, and selected insert must be compatible.

Some projects may require a chimney inspection or venting modification before the insert can be installed.

Explore Fireplace Insert Installation

Can I use the existing chimney with a replacement fireplace?

In some cases, an existing chimney can remain in use with a compatible replacement fireplace.

However, a different appliance may have different venting or liner requirements. The chimney and selected unit should be reviewed together before installation is approved.

Explore Chimney Inspections

Are gas, electrical, chimney work, and permits included?

Not automatically. Some projects require gas connections, electrical work, chimney modifications, building changes, or permits.
