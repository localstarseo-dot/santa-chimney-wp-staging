What Happens During Fireplace Cleaning?

Santa Chimney Sweep addresses ash, soot, and loose residue inside the fireplace areas covered by the service. Cleaning methods depend on the fireplace type, surface materials, and buildup found.

Step 1

Review the Fireplace

Our technicians review the fireplace type and accessible areas before cleaning begins.

They identify visible ash, soot, loose residue, and areas that may need special handling.

If cracked materials, damaged panels, or other visible problems are found, these are considered separately from routine cleaning.

01
Step 2

Protect the Surrounding Area

Before cleaning, technicians protect the surrounding hearth and work area to help keep soot and debris from spreading into the room.

Dust-control equipment and suitable cleaning methods help contain residue while the work is performed.

02
Step 3

Clean the Agreed Surfaces

Technicians remove ash, soot, and loose debris from suitable fireplace surfaces included in the approved cleaning service.

The work may involve the firebox floor, interior walls, or other accessible components, depending on the fireplace type.

Glass, gas logs, decorative materials, and other components require appropriate methods and must be included in the agreed service scope.

03
Step 4

Review the Cleaned Areas

After cleaning, the team reviews the accessible areas covered by the service.

Some permanent stains, discoloration, or damaged materials may remain because cleaning cannot restore every surface to its original appearance.

If another concern is identified, technicians explain whether a separate inspection, repair, or chimney sweeping service may be appropriate.

04

Questions Homeowners Ask About Fireplace Cleaning

Is fireplace cleaning the same as chimney sweeping?

No. Fireplace cleaning focuses on ash, soot, and residue in the fireplace area, while chimney sweeping addresses buildup inside the chimney flue.

Both services can be requested when needed, but one should not be assumed to include the other.

Explore Chimney Sweeping

Can gas logs, glass, and other components be cleaned?

Some fireplace components require specialized cleaning methods.

Santa Chimney Sweep has completed gas-log cleaning and decorative-material service, but the exact work available depends on the appliance, materials, and agreed service scope.

Tell our team which components need attention when booking.

Will cleaning remove smoky odors?

Cleaning may reduce odors caused by ash, soot, or residue inside the fireplace.

However, smells caused by moisture, chimney buildup, or venting problems may require further evaluation.
