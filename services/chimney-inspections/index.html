What Happens During a Chimney Inspection?

Santa Chimney Sweep provides Level 1 and Level 2 chimney inspections. Each level has a different scope based on the chimney's condition, how it is used, and the reason for the inspection.

Step 1

Choose the Inspection Level

A Level 1 inspection is appropriate for routine maintenance when the chimney and connected appliance will continue operating under the same conditions.

A Level 2 inspection is more detailed and is generally needed when a property is sold or transferred, the appliance or fuel type changes, a chimney is being relined, or an event may have caused damage.

01
Step 2

Level 1: Readily Accessible Areas

Our technicians visually examine readily accessible portions of the chimney, fireplace or connected appliance, and chimney connection.

The inspection checks for visible structural damage, obstructions, and combustible deposits such as soot and creosote.

A Level 1 inspection does not require removing permanently attached building components or opening concealed spaces.

02
Step 3

Level 2: Detailed Examination

A Level 2 inspection includes the Level 1 checks and a more detailed examination of the chimney system.

Technicians examine additional accessible areas, including relevant attic, basement, or crawl-space locations where safe access is available.

The inspection includes an internal video scan or equivalent examination of flue-liner surfaces to look for cracks, gaps, missing joints, or other visible defects.

A Level 2 inspection does not involve tearing open walls or removing permanent building structures to reach concealed areas.

03
Step 4

Review Findings and Next Steps

After the inspection, technicians explain the conditions observed and any areas that need attention.

If soot buildup, a damaged liner, masonry deterioration, or another concern is identified, the team explains whether cleaning, repair, or further evaluation is recommended.

Any additional service is discussed before the homeowner approves the work.

04

Questions Homeowners Ask About Chimney Inspections

Do I need a Level 1 or Level 2 inspection?

A Level 1 inspection is generally suitable for routine checks when your chimney and fireplace system have not changed.

A Level 2 inspection is appropriate for property transfers, changes to the appliance or fuel, planned relining, or events that may have damaged the chimney.

Does a Level 2 inspection include a video scan?

Yes. A Level 2 inspection includes a video scan or equivalent examination of the interior flue liner.

This helps identify visible cracks, gaps, missing tile joints, and other damage that may be difficult to see during a basic inspection.

Can an inspection find every concealed problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every concealed defect will be found.

Level 1 focuses on readily accessible components, while Level 2 examines additional accessible areas and the flue interior. If a serious problem is suspected behind concealed structures, further evaluation may be necessary.

What happens if the inspection finds a concern?

Our technicians explain the identified concern and whether cleaning, further inspection, or repair may be needed.

If separate work is recommended, the proposed service is reviewed with you before you decide whether to approve it.
